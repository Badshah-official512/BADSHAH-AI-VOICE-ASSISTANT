Privacy Policy — BADSHAH AI

Effective: 18 November 2026 · Developer: Zain Shah (Badshah), Swabi, Pakistan · Contact: zainff043@gmail.com · WhatsApp: +92 325 1787969

BADSHAH AI ("the app") is a voice assistant available as a website (badshah222-ai.pages.dev) and an Android app. This policy explains what information the app uses and why — in plain words.

1. Account information

If you create an account, sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google). We receive your email address and display name so the app can show your profile and protect paid/owner features. Guest mode works without an account for chat and images.

2. Chats, voice and images

  • Chat messages you type are sent through our server to AI providers (Google Gemini and Groq) to generate replies. Chat history shown in the app is stored locally on your device/browser.
  • Voice calls: when you start a call, your microphone audio is streamed to Google Gemini Live, or — in ElevenLabs mode — converted to text (Groq Whisper), answered by the AI, and spoken back using ElevenLabs text-to-speech. Audio is processed to run the call; we do not sell your audio or messages.
  • Images you ask the app to create are generated by AI providers (Google Gemini / Pollinations) from your text prompt.

3. Device permissions (Android app)

The app may ask for permissions only when a feature needs them: Microphone (voice calls and voice messages), Camera and Photos/Videos (image features), Contacts (calling a contact by name), Calendar (reminders you ask for), Location (location-based answers), Notifications (announcements and replies), Bluetooth/Nearby devices (audio devices). You can deny or revoke any permission in Android Settings at any time; the related feature simply will not work.

4. Analytics

We keep basic, aggregated usage counts (for example, number of visits and feature use) in our own database to improve the app. We do not use third-party advertising trackers, and we never sell personal data.

5. API keys

AI provider keys belong to the developer and are stored securely on our server (Supabase). They are never part of your data and are not shared with other users.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

7. Your choices & deletion

You can use guest mode without an account, clear your local chat history from the app at any time, and ask us to delete your account data by emailing zainff043@gmail.com. We will complete deletion requests within 30 days.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
