Is BADSHAH AI really free?

Yes — completely. All chat models, image generation, Groq models, live calls and premium voices are free. There is no paid plan at all. For live calls you only add your own free Gemini key (Google AI Studio gives it free).

How do live calls work?

Calls run on Gemini 3.8 Live. You add your own free Gemini key once (from Google AI Studio — the app shows you exactly how), then just log in and press Call. BADSHAH (voice: Charon) talks with real feelings — he listens first and answers after.

Can BADSHAH control my phone and computer?

On Android, the BADSHAH AI app can open apps, message, call and browse for you, and can be your default digital assistant. On the web you can pair a computer with a code and control it through BADSHAH.

Do I need my own API key?

Only for live voice calls — and that key is free: Google AI Studio gives every user a free Gemini key, and the app guides you to add it in two minutes. Chat and images need no key from you at all — they run on the service's own keys.
